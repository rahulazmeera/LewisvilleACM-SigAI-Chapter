About the chapter

The Lewisville ACM SIGAI Chapter is an officially recognized chapter of the Association for Computing Machinery and its Special Interest Group on Artificial Intelligence. Our mission is to make AI education accessible, hands-on, and community-driven.

Whether you are writing your first line of Python or fine-tuning transformer models, there is a place for you here. We host talks, run project teams, prepare for competitions, and connect members with mentors and career opportunities across the Dallas–Fort Worth technology community.

  • LearnWorkshops and study groups on ML, deep learning, and LLMs.
  • BuildSemester-long project teams shipping real AI applications.
  • CompeteHackathons, Kaggle competitions, and AI challenge events.
  • ConnectGuest speakers, industry mentors, and a supportive peer network.

What we do

Technical Workshops

Beginner-friendly, hands-on sessions covering Python for ML, neural networks, computer vision, NLP, and prompt engineering.

Project Teams

Join a small team and build something real over a semester — from chatbots and recommender systems to robotics and research replications.

Speaker Series

Talks from researchers, industry engineers, and founders working on the frontier of applied and responsible AI.

Competitions

We form teams for hackathons, Kaggle challenges, and university AI competitions, with practice sessions leading up to each.

Reading Group

A relaxed paper-discussion group working through influential and recent papers in machine learning.

Career Support

Resume reviews, mock interviews, internship referrals, and portfolio feedback focused on AI/ML roles.
